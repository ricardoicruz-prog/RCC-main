## Audience
- **Primary Audience**: Founder-led service businesses, **10–50 employees** (confirmed range — see Positioning/Engagement Fit below; supersedes the earlier "5–30" figure from Owen's persona draft, which is now out of date).
- **Key Problem**: Work is visibly stuck in approvals, handoffs, reporting, follow-up, routing, onboarding, or recurring admin. The founder is the bottleneck, or a key employee's tribal knowledge is a single point of failure.
- **Their Language** (verbatim, from persona voice banks and published case studies):
  - "I cannot keep doing this."
  - "I don't even know how we'd take on more customers without everything falling apart."
  - "I keep hiring people to take things off my plate. Somehow my plate is always full."
  - "Nobody has ever asked if there's a better way." (admin-staff variant)

## Positioning
- **Differentiation**: Enterprise-caliber operational design — 15+ years at Fidelity Investments, MBA with Distinction — delivered at SMB-accessible speed and price. Core thesis, in Ricardo's own words: *"Operational pain is a process problem — not a people problem and not a technology problem. I build the systems that prove that."* Supporting proof: a 5-day audit and a 2-week first build, versus the months/quarters typical of enterprise consulting engagements.
- **Competitors**: Not formally benchmarked. Ricardo's own assessment: the consultants/firms he's identified in this space are either very small (solo operators) or very large and established (traditional enterprise consultancies) — RCC doesn't map cleanly onto either, which makes direct benchmarking hard right now.
- **Market Position**: Niche/premium — positioned between low-cost solo tool-implementers and expensive enterprise consultancies. The pitch is Fortune 500 methodology at a price and timeline an owner-operator can actually say yes to.
- **Engagement Fit (qualification gate, Ricardo's own framework):**
  - 🟢 **Green — strong fit**: Founder-led service business, 10–50 employees, work visibly stuck in approvals, handoffs, reporting, follow-up, routing, onboarding, or recurring admin. The founder is the bottleneck, or a key employee's tribal knowledge is a single point of failure.
  - 🟡 **Yellow — scope first**: Professional services or compliance-sensitive firm outside the core range but with clear founder-dependence symptoms. Or: client wants automation but the process hasn't been mapped yet — propose process mapping before any build.
  - 🔴 **Red — likely not a fit**: Early-stage startup with no real operating complexity yet (needs growth help, not operational redesign). A pure "tell us which tool to use" request with no underlying bottleneck. A business unwilling to fix a broken process but wanting automation layered on top of it anyway.

## Brand Personality
- **Personality Words**: calm, direct, consultative, credentialed, plain-spoken
- **Tone**: Professional but warm, never urgent, pushy, or salesy. High-pedigree credentials (MBA, Fidelity, President's Circle) presented in a low-key, non-braggy, plain-spoken style rather than leading with them. Short sentences carry the core idea; longer ones explain context. Specific over vague ("90% escalation reduction," not "significant improvement"). No em dashes (max one per document). No "it's not X, it's Y" constructs. No buzzwords: scalable, leverage, synergy, cutting-edge, game-changing, upstream, enterprise.
