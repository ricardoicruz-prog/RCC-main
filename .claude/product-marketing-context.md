## Product Overview
**One-liner:** "I help growing businesses eliminate the manual work that is quietly costing them time and revenue — using AI and automation tools that pay for themselves within weeks of deployment."
**What it does:** Audits operations, then designs and deploys the automations (workflow, tool integrations, documentation, training) that remove the founder as the operational bottleneck.
**Product category:** AI/automation operations consulting — how the audience searches for it: "business consultant for [my industry]," "how to stop being the bottleneck in my business," "operations audit for small business."
**Product type:** Service (consulting engagement), tiered by scope. Entry point is a paid diagnostic (Tier 1 Audit), not a free lead magnet — [NOTE: this supersedes the earlier draft, which assumed a free "Operational Capacity Audit" lead magnet; the actual funnel entry point is the $1,500–$2,000 paid audit].
**Business model:** Five tiers, all beginning with a signed MSA + Statement of Work.

| Tier | Name | Price | Format | Timeline | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AI Operations Audit | $1,500–$2,000 | One-time, no ongoing commitment | 5 business days | Owners who know something's broken but don't know where to start; 10–50 person teams |
| 2 Lite | Focused Implementation | $3,500–$5,000 | Project, 50% deposit to start | 2 weeks | 10–20 employees, one clear painful workflow |
| 2 Standard | Done-For-You Implementation | $8,500–$10,000 | Project, 50% deposit / 50% at delivery | 4 weeks | 25–50 employees, multiple broken workflows ("most popular" tier) |
| 2 Complex | Multi-System Implementation | $12,000–$18,000 | Project, milestone schedule | 5–6 weeks | 30–50+ employees, cross-departmental complexity |
| 3 | Fractional AI Operations Retainer | $3,500–$6,000/mo | Monthly, billed on the 1st, 30-day cancellation notice | Ongoing | Post-Tier-2 clients wanting ongoing optimization; positioned against a $120K–$180K/yr in-house hire |

**Natural progression:** Audit → Lite or Standard implementation → (often expands to cover more workflows) → Retainer. The Audit includes a $500–$1,000 conversion credit toward implementation if the client moves forward within 30 days — the audit findings become the SOW, so there's no starting over.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Founder-led service businesses, 10–50 employees, across (at minimum) three active outreach verticals: mental health/counseling practices, medical practices (dental, dermatology, eye care, urgent care, PT, chiropractic, OB/GYN), and wellness/fitness (gyms, studios, recovery centers, yoga).
**Decision-makers:** Owner/Founder/CEO — budget holder and final decision-maker (Owen persona). Internal champion: first ops hire or COO (Chloe). Bottom-up signal: office/admin manager (Sam).
**Primary use case:** Removing the founder as the single point of failure in daily operations.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Get out of daily firefighting mode and back to strategic work
- Build systems the team can run without the owner's constant input
- Make room to grow without the operational foundation collapsing

**Engagement fit gate (Ricardo's own qualification framework — use this instead of a generic persona filter):**
- 🟢 **Green (strong fit):** Founder-led service business, 10–50 employees, work visibly stuck in approvals, handoffs, reporting, follow-up, routing, onboarding, or recurring admin. Founder is the bottleneck, or a key employee's tribal knowledge is a single point of failure.
- 🟡 **Yellow (scope first):** Professional services or compliance-sensitive firm outside the core range but with clear founder-dependence symptoms. Or: client wants automation but the process hasn't been mapped yet — propose process mapping before any build.
- 🔴 **Red (not a fit):** Early-stage startup with no real operating complexity yet (needs growth help, not operational redesign). A pure "tell us which tool to use" request with no underlying bottleneck. A business unwilling to fix a broken process but wanting automation layered on top of it anyway.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Revenue and complexity are growing faster than the operational foundation. The owner (or a single key employee) is the single point of failure.
**Why alternatives fall short:** Hiring more staff without fixing the underlying systems adds coordination overhead instead of removing it.
**What it costs them:** Concrete, case-study-backed examples —
- A day spa owner: 20 hrs/week on manual admin (sticky-note orders, manual inventory checks, end-of-day QuickBooks entry), 3 late nights/week, **$150K in annual service capacity blocked** by admin instead of billable service hours.
- A boutique gym owner: 10 hrs/week manually reviewing client data and writing feedback by hand, **$72K in annual training capacity** consumed by admin only he could do.
**Emotional tension:** Close to being broken by a business that isn't itself broken. Guilt, fatigue, and the fear that letting go of control will break something.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Enterprise-caliber methodology — 15+ years of enterprise operations experience at Fidelity Investments, MBA with Distinction — applied to businesses that could never otherwise access that caliber of operational thinking
- Speed: 5-day audit, first live automation in 2 weeks (vs. the months/quarters typical of enterprise consulting)
- ROI made explicit: every audit recommendation ships with a calculated cost-savings estimate; real example — $41/month in new tool costs recovered $150K in annual capacity, paid back within the first week
- Full ownership handover at close: every credential, API key, SOP, and Loom walkthrough transferred to the client — no lock-in
**How we do it differently:** Diagnose first (paid audit maps every bottleneck and prioritizes by ROI), then build only what's proven to matter — not a generic tech-stack sale.
**Why that's better:** The client owns the systems and the documentation, not just the outcome.
**Why customers choose us:** *"Operational pain is a process problem — not a people problem and not a technology problem. I build the systems that prove that."*

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Chaos growing faster than revenue; can't step away; hiring already tried and didn't solve it; team making avoidable mistakes from undocumented process.
**Pull:** Real, quantified time and revenue recovery — "10 hours back," $150K in unblocked capacity, tools that pay for themselves in weeks.
**Habit:** Comfort/control of doing everything themselves; belief that no one else can do it the way they would.
**Anxiety:** Fear that delegating or systematizing will break something that currently (barely) works.
