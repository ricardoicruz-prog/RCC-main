## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Operational systems consulting that gives overwhelmed SMB owners their time back.
**What it does:** Audits and redesigns the operational systems (scheduling, intake, billing, onboarding, membership tracking — specifics vary by vertical) that force every decision through the owner, so the business can run without them in the room.
**Product category:** Small business operations consulting / "fractional operations design" — how RCC's audience would search: "business consultant for [my industry]," "how to stop being the bottleneck in my business," "operations audit for small business."
**Product type:** Service (consulting engagement), not software. Entry point is a free lead magnet: the Operational Capacity Audit (10 minutes, maps where time is lost) at ricardocruz.io.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** SMBs in three verticals — mental health/counseling practices, medical practices (dental, dermatology, eye care, urgent care, digestive health, podiatry, PT, chiropractic, OB/GYN & women's health), and wellness/fitness (gyms, studios, recovery/performance centers, yoga). 5–30 employees, $1M–$5M revenue, 3–10 years in business.
**Decision-makers:** Owner/Founder/CEO is the budget holder and final decision-maker (see Personas below).
**Primary use case:** Removing founder dependency — the owner can't step away without things breaking, despite (or because of) growth.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Get out of daily firefighting mode and back to strategic work
- Build systems the team can run without the owner's constant input
- Make room to grow (more customers, bigger clients) without the operational foundation collapsing
**Use cases:** Scheduling and intake bottlenecks, insurance verification and billing chaos, patient/client onboarding admin, membership/session tracking, undocumented processes that live only in the owner's head.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Chaos growing faster than revenue; can't step away; hiring already tried and didn't solve it; team making avoidable mistakes from undocumented process.
**Pull:** "10 hours back" — real rest, the business running without the owner in the room, bandwidth for strategic work and bigger clients.
**Habit:** Comfort/control of doing everything themselves; belief that no one else can do it the way they would; sunk cost in the current (broken) way of operating.
**Anxiety:** Fear that delegating or systematizing will break something that currently (barely) works; distrust of consultants who don't understand their specific industry; cost of getting it wrong.
